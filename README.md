# 🍔 Crib Burgers

**Crib Burgers** is a modern online food ordering experience built to make ordering delicious, freshly made burgers fast and simple.  
From signature stacked burgers to classic favorites, Crib Burgers brings flavor, speed, and convenience together — straight from the crib to your doorstep.

---

## 🚀 About Crib Burgers

Crib Burgers started as a passion for perfectly seasoned beef and chicken, bold flavors, and quality ingredients. What began in the kitchen has grown into a brand focused on:

- Freshly prepared burgers
- Signature sauces and custom seasoning
- Fast ordering via WhatsApp and phone
- A clean, modern online ordering experience

Every burger is crafted with care — because at Crib Burgers, **you can’t eat only one**.

---

## 🛒 Features

- 🍔 Interactive burger menu
- 🛍️ Add-to-cart ordering system
- 📱 Order directly via WhatsApp
- 📞 Call-to-order support
